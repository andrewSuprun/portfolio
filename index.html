Why teams work with me

What you get when I join a project.

One person, the whole store

Theme, product and cart UX, apps, integrations and launch. You don't need a separate frontend and backend developer for a Shopify project.

Integrations that don't break

I come from financial systems: car auctions with deposits, payments and real-time bidding. Webhooks are verified, queued and idempotent, so an order is never processed twice.

Questions before code

I go through the brief and ask about the edge cases first: markets, bundles, subscriptions, returns. That saves rework after launch.

Fast delivery

Modern tooling, AI-assisted development and Figma-to-theme workflow. A product page or a new section ships in days, not weeks.

Easy to work with
